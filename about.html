Biography

The work.

Matthew Suess is a photographer with the Marketing department at Franciscan University of Steubenville. The university work covers campus life and the pictures that carry the school’s story online and in print.

He also photographs Franciscan athletics — the Men’s Tennis team and the Women’s Basketball team — and works independently for clients.

With Terra Bona Family he makes narrative films: Catherine, on St. Catherine Labouré and the Miraculous Medal; Zita, on St. Zita of Lucca; and Vianney: Curé of Ars, now in production, with the trailer already out.

In 2024 he edited about one hundred YouTube Shorts for Star Wars Insider, work that has drawn millions of views.

Away from campus he has shot IMSA with a media credential: the Rolex 24 At Daytona and the race at Watkins Glen.
