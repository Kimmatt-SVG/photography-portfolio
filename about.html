Biography

The studio.

Kim Matt is a photographer working between portrait, landscape, and the still interior. The pictures favor patience over spectacle: available light, a slow sitting, a place allowed to remain itself.

Work is edited into named series rather than shown as a dump of files. Each chapter is a sitting, a coastline, a house, or an hour of the city — sequenced the way a book is sequenced, with room around the frame.

Commissions, editorials, and print inquiries are welcome. The studio travels.
